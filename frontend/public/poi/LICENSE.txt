chicTrip Japan / Korea / Taiwan POI data

Scope: 74 curated travel-area bounding boxes, not a complete national inventory, ranking, or precise administrative boundary dataset. Overlapping region records are intentional; national exports deduplicate by OSM ID. See index.json and exports/report.json for observed counts and collection times.

POI geographic data: copyright OpenStreetMap contributors. Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Wikidata labels and descriptions: CC0.
https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing

Photos: Wikimedia Commons, Wikipedia, linked websites and public image search results. Each record preserves its image URL, original URL, source page, source credit and any available license. Web-source images without a verified image license are marked licenseStatus: unspecified and do not carry a Creative Commons license. A search result does not grant reuse permission; image rights remain with the source rights holder. Retain source credits and follow source terms when reusing images. Large web photos may have a mobile JPEG thumbnail in photos/; thumbnailOf preserves its source URL. Full-size originals are not included in this package. Null photo means a photo has not been verified; imageStatus distinguishes unavailable and pending. Missing source attributes remain null.

GeoJSON coordinates are [longitude, latitude], WGS84. CSV is UTF-8 with BOM; formula-like values are prefixed with an apostrophe for safe opening in spreadsheet applications.
